# Direct RT validation, 2026-10-28

The actual native-source pilot completed two independent IC seeds, 111 and 222,
with 41 snapshots from z=20 to 4. Sources were generated by compiled 21cmFAST, not
synthetic substitutes. The five direct RT configurations per seed all completed.
`local_validation.json` records inputs, hashes and compact measured results.

The maximum independently recomputed photon-ledger relative residual across
all saved outputs was **4.5603e-14**. The maximum independent discrepancy between
the saved cumulative source field and the trapezoidal native-rate integral was
**1.1026e-16**. In-flight radiation is retained in the physical photon inventory.

All comparisons below use the same native 8-cubed analysis grid and every
populated shell within 0.1 <= k <= 0.6 times its Nyquist frequency. The box is
50 cMpc; the two selected k values are 0.16036 and 0.28033 cMpc^-1. The active stage
is 0.1 <= volume-mean reference xHI < 0.9.

Time-step sensitivity passes the 1% per-bin criterion on the tested grid and
directions. Angular and spatial sensitivity do not. These outputs therefore
remain **uncertified training labels**; ledger precision is not P21 precision.
Source-history sampling has not been independently converged by changing CFL.

The mean matched ES versus finest RT P21 differences are 24.19% and 13.17% for the
two seeds. These are model differences, not trained NN errors or isolated
photon-loss measurements. The RT also includes finite propagation and opacity
that the ES threshold does not resolve.

An independent continuum benchmark propagated a collimated sinusoid over a
quarter periodic box with optical depth 0.7. Relative cell-average L1 errors
at 8, 16, 32, 64 cells per axis were 6.0245%, 3.2435%, 1.6902%, 0.8637%, demonstrating
the expected first-order spatial convergence. The measured mean attenuation
was 0.496585303801 versus the analytic 0.496585303791. This tests streaming and
absorption together, separately from photon bookkeeping.

The current physical model is explicitly monochromatic, fixed-density-contrast,
single effective absorber, without recombinations, thermal feedback or photon
frequency redshifting. It uses the restricted native L-INTEGRAL source model.
These successful runs do not establish a full Qin20 or multifrequency RT model.

The targeted suite passed **37 tests**. Ruff, Bash syntax and diff-whitespace
checks passed. These are software checks, separate from the numerical results above.

The Gadi preparation and frozen PBS job body were also executed locally with the
actual compiled native backend: one seed, all 41 snapshots and all five RT
configurations completed, including independent output audits. This checks the
runner end to end; it is not a submitted Gadi job.
